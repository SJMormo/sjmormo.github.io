About Me

Ph.D. Applicant, Fall 2027 Undergrad in CSE, RUET Former R&D Engineer @ Shanghai BDCOM

My long-term research goal is to advance the development of intelligent systems that can learn, perceive, and interact with the real world in a more robust and human-like manner, bridging the gap between theory and practical applications.

I consider myself resilient and self-motivated. I enjoy taking on new challenges and continuously improving my skills. With curiosity and determination, I aim to grow as both a researcher and an engineer.
